The details

Six things about how Nowa is built.

2-minute missions

A narrator reads each mission out loud, and the pet plays it with your child. Most take about two minutes.

Play that ends

The pet gets tired and goes to sleep. Sessions close on their own, without a fight over one more tap.

Nothing to scroll

No feed, no autoplay, no open internet. The device runs the pet and its missions, and nothing else.

A mic with a switch

It listens only while your child holds the talk button. Speech is read on the device, the audio is deleted, and a physical switch turns the mic off.

You set the limits

Missions, time limits, and bedtime mode live in the parent app, next to a record of what your child practiced.

Grows 3 to 10

Missions re-tune to your child's age band, so the same pet fits a 4-year-old and a 9-year-old.

When does it ship, and what if it's late?+

Can I cancel my reservation?+

Anytime before your Nowa ships, for any reason, and we refund every cent. Your reservation is never a commitment to keep it.

Does it talk to my child?+

No. A narrator reads the missions; the pet answers in sound, motion, and feeling. It never speaks a sentence, and that's deliberate: your child does the imagining, and there's no chatbot to monitor.

Is this just another screen?+

It's a 2.4-inch, low-stimulation screen with no feed, no autoplay, and no open internet. Check-ins run a few minutes, and the pet gets tired and sleeps, so play ends on its own. The whole design points your child back to the real world.

What ages is it right for?+

Ages 3 to 10. Missions re-tune to your child's developmental band, so a 4-year-old and a 9-year-old get different versions of the same pet. It's built to be a multi-year companion.

Does it need a subscription?+

No. The pet, the Care Loop, routines, and core missions come with the device. Nowa+ is optional, adds advanced missions and full insights, and your bundle includes six months of it free. After that it's $20 a month, and reserving now locks that price for two years.

What about the microphone?+

It's push-to-talk: it listens only while your child holds the button. Speech is read on the device, the audio is deleted, and there's a physical switch that turns the mic off entirely. We built it so you never have to take our word for it.
